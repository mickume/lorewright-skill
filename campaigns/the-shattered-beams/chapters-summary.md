# Complete Chapter Summaries

**How to Use This Document**: This provides scene-level overview for all chapters of the campaign. For detailed scene-by-scene breakdowns with full stat blocks, dialog suggestions, and skill checks, see the individual chapter files (chapter-01.md, etc.). Use these summaries for campaign planning and quick reference.

# [Chapter 1: The Drawing](chapter-01.md)
**Session 1 | Level 4**

**Full Detailed Breakdown**: See [chapter-01.md](chapter-01.md)

**Summary**: The PCs are wrenched through malfunctioning Doors at pivotal moments in their lives and deposited at the Waystation — a pocket of stable reality maintained by Aldric Vane. Disoriented and suspicious, they learn they are bound by the Constant and that the world outside is dying. A Thinny breach at the Waystation's perimeter forces them to fight together for the first time.

**Key Scenes**:
- Individual Door vignettes for each PC (collaborative flashbacks)
- Waking at the Waystation, meeting Aldric Vane and each other
- Aldric's exposition: the Fracturing, the Beams, the Constant
- Thinny breach at the perimeter — first combat encounter

**NPCs:** [Aldric Vane](npcs.md#aldric-vane)

**Locations:** [The Waystation](locations.md#the-waystation)

**Encounters**:
- **The Breach** (combat): Aberrant creatures pour through a crack in the Waystation's barrier. The party must seal the breach while fighting off a Gibbering Mouther and 2 Nothics. Aldric provides support by maintaining the barrier but cannot fight.

**Quests**:
- Reach the nearest settlement (Loom) to find answers about the Beams
- Follow the pull of the Constant toward the nearest active Beam

**Rewards**:
- Aldric's supplies: basic adventuring gear, a map of the Ashlands (outdated), and trail rations
- Understanding of the Constant bond (shared dream mechanic, phantom pain awareness)

**Hooks**: Aldric tells the party that the Covenant of the Pylon has a presence in Loom and can explain more about the Beams. He cannot leave the Waystation — someone must maintain the barrier.

# [Chapter 2: The Blighted Way](chapter-02.md)
**Session 2 | Level 4**

**Full Detailed Breakdown**: See [chapter-02.md](chapter-02.md)

**Summary**: The party travels through the Ashlands, a blighted wilderness where reality frays at the edges. They encounter the wreckage of a crashed skyship, a temporal anomaly that loops a battlefield endlessly, and a dormant warforged named Moth who carries fragmentary memories of the Old Ones. In a magitech ruin, they recover a chrono-compass — a device that points not just to where, but to *when*.

**Key Scenes**:
- Journey through the Ashlands: environmental storytelling and survival
- The Skyship Graveyard: exploration of a crashed magitech vessel
- The Endless Field: a temporal loop battlefield, skill challenge to escape
- The Ruin of Vethara: discovery of Moth and the chrono-compass

**NPCs:** [Moth](npcs.md#moth)

**Locations:** [The Ashlands](locations.md#the-ashlands)

**Encounters**:
- **The Skyship Graveyard** (exploration): A crashed skyship contains salvageable magitech components and a log crystal that plays the captain's final moments — the levitation engine failed mid-flight as the Beam it drew power from destabilized.
- **The Endless Field** (skill challenge/hybrid): A temporal anomaly traps the party in a battlefield that replays the same battle endlessly. Complexity 2 (6 successes before 3 failures). DC 13-15 Arcana, Investigation, Survival, Athletics. Success: they escape with knowledge of temporal mechanics. Failure: they escape but each PC suffers one level of exhaustion.
- **The Ruin of Vethara** (exploration/combat): An Old Ones facility where Moth lies dormant. Activating Moth triggers the ruin's security — 2 Animated Armors and 1 Helmed Horror.

**Quests**:
- Reach Loom (continuing from Chapter 1)
- Optional: Repair Moth's memory crystal to unlock more of the Old Ones' history

**Rewards**:
- Chrono-compass (uncommon wondrous item)
- Moth as a companion NPC
- Skyship salvage: 2 healing potions, 50 gp in magitech components
- Milestone: level up to 5 at chapter end

**Hooks**: The chrono-compass pulls toward Loom — not just its location, but a specific *time*. Moth's fragmentary memories mention Breaker Stations and the Covenant.

# [Chapter 3: Loom](chapter-03.md)
**Session 3 | Level 5**

**Full Detailed Breakdown**: See [chapter-03.md](chapter-03.md)

**Summary**: The party arrives at Loom, a city that phases between time periods — sometimes medieval, sometimes gleaming with Old Ones architecture, sometimes crumbling ruin. They navigate its unstable reality, meet Elder Soraya (the settlement's pragmatic leader), and make contact with the remnants of the Covenant of the Pylon. Through investigation, social encounters, and exploration of Loom's phasing districts, they learn about the Unravelers, the Breaker Stations, and the location of the nearest one: Terminus.

**Key Scenes**:
- Arrival at Loom: the city phases as they approach, showing three versions of itself
- Meeting Elder Soraya at the Council Hall
- The Covenant cell: meeting Keeper Jorin and learning the faction's history
- The Phasing Market: a marketplace that exists in multiple time periods simultaneously, where merchants sell goods from different eras
- Investigation: gathering intelligence on the Breaker Station's location and defenses

**NPCs:** [Elder Soraya](npcs.md#elder-soraya), Keeper Jorin (minor NPC, Covenant contact)

**Locations:** [Loom](locations.md#loom)

**Encounters**:
- **The Council Hall** (social): Elder Soraya is suspicious of outsiders — especially ones who can perceive the Beams. She needs convincing (DC 14 Persuasion or demonstration of the Constant bond) before she'll share information. Her secret: she's been negotiating with the Unravelers to spare Loom in exchange for non-interference.
- **The Phasing Market** (exploration/social): The market phases between eras. Players can buy goods from the past or future, but must navigate temporal disorientation (DC 12 Wisdom saves or become briefly confused). Contains a Covenant dead drop with intelligence on the Breaker Station.
- **The Covenant Cell** (social): Keeper Jorin provides the location of Breaker Station Terminus and what little the Covenant knows about its defenses. He asks the party to shut it down — but warns that the Breakers cannot simply be freed without consequences.

**Quests**:
- Gain Elder Soraya's trust and learn what she knows
- Make contact with the Covenant cell
- Locate Breaker Station Terminus
- Optional: Discover Soraya's secret deal with the Unravelers

**Rewards**:
- Covenant supplies: 2 potions of healing, rope of climbing, map of the Breaker Station approach
- Intelligence on Breaker Station Terminus (guard rotations, entry points, the Breaker chamber layout)
- Optional: If Soraya's deal is exposed, she provides additional resources to prove her loyalty

**Hooks**: The Covenant's intelligence indicates the Breaker Station is actively eroding the fifth Beam. Time is running out. Keeper Jorin warns that the Threadcutter has been seen in the region.

# [Chapter 4: The Breaker Station](chapter-04.md)
**Sessions 4-5 | Level 5**

**Full Detailed Breakdown**: See [chapter-04.md](chapter-04.md)

**Summary**: The party infiltrates Breaker Station Terminus — an underground facility where captive psychics are strapped into magitech chairs, their minds weaponized against the Beams. They navigate trapped corridors, fight Unraveler guards, and reach the Breaker Chamber where they find Wren — a young, terrified psychic of immense power. Freeing the Breakers causes a dangerous energy surge that destabilizes the Beam further. Leaving them means allowing the slow destruction to continue. There is no clean answer.

**Key Scenes**:
- Approach and infiltration of the station (multiple entry points)
- The Guard Barracks: combat or stealth past Unraveler soldiers
- The Processing Hall: discovery of the magitech chairs and what they do
- The Breaker Chamber: meeting Wren, the moral decision
- The Surge: consequences of the party's choice

**NPCs:** [Wren](npcs.md#wren), Unraveler guards (minor NPCs)

**Locations:** [Breaker Station Terminus](locations.md#breaker-station-terminus)

**Encounters**:
- **The Guard Barracks** (combat/stealth): 4 Unraveler soldiers (Veterans, MM p.350) and 1 Unraveler Channeler (Mage, MM p.347). Can be bypassed with stealth (DC 15 group check) or engaged directly.
- **The Processing Hall** (exploration/horror): The chairs are occupied by semiconscious psychics. Examining them reveals the mechanism: their psychic energy is being channeled through the facility's magitech to create an interference pattern that erodes the Beam. DC 16 Arcana to understand the system; DC 18 to understand the consequences of disruption.
- **The Breaker Chamber** (social/moral): Wren is lucid enough to communicate. She knows what's happening to her and begs for release. But she also knows that a sudden disconnection will cause a surge — potentially destabilizing the Beam and creating a Thinny storm. Three options: free them (surge), leave them (moral cost), or attempt a controlled shutdown (DC 18 Arcana check, failure causes the surge anyway).

**Quests**:
- Shut down Breaker Station Terminus
- Decide the Breakers' fate
- Learn the Threadcutter's identity and plan

**Rewards**:
- Beam-Shard Amulet (rare wondrous item, found in the station commander's quarters)
- Wren as an NPC ally (if freed)
- The Threadcutter's identity: Sera Mourne, former Covenant scholar
- Intelligence: the Threadcutter is heading for the Eld Pylon of Keth
- Milestone: level up to 6 at chapter end

**Hooks**: Whether or not the Breakers are freed, the station's disruption sends ripples through the region. A Thinny storm begins forming, heading directly for Loom.

# [Chapter 5: The Thinny Storm](chapter-05.md)
**Session 5-6 | Level 6**

**Full Detailed Breakdown**: See [chapter-05.md](chapter-05.md)

**Summary**: A massive Thinny storm — triggered or accelerated by the events at the Breaker Station — is bearing down on Loom. The party must organize the city's defense or evacuation while the storm's edge tears reality apart. Aberrant creatures pour through the rents in the world. The ticking clock forces a choice: stay and save Loom's people, or leave now to intercept the Threadcutter at the Eld Pylon before she can destroy the fifth Beam.

**Key Scenes**:
- Return to Loom: the storm is visible on the horizon, a wall of shimmering wrongness
- Emergency council with Elder Soraya: organize defense or evacuation
- The storm's edge arrives: combat with Thinny-spawned aberrations
- The choice: stay to finish the evacuation or pursue the Threadcutter

**NPCs:** [Elder Soraya](npcs.md#elder-soraya), [Wren](npcs.md#wren) (if present)

**Locations:** [Loom](locations.md#loom), [The Ashlands](locations.md#the-ashlands)

**Encounters**:
- **Emergency Council** (social/ticking clock): Elder Soraya needs the party's help to organize a defense or evacuation. Time is short — 1 hour of in-game time before the storm hits. If Soraya's secret deal was exposed, she's desperate to prove herself. If not, she's calm but grim.
- **The Storm's Edge** (combat/siege): Waves of aberrations: Wave 1 (2 Gibbering Mouthers, 4 Cranium Rats), Wave 2 (1 Otyugh, 2 Nothics), Wave 3 (1 Chuul, 2 Phase Spiders). Each wave arrives 3 rounds apart. The party holds a chokepoint while civilians flee.
- **The Choice** (narrative): After Wave 2, a messenger arrives: the Threadcutter has been spotted approaching the Eld Pylon. If the party leaves now, they can intercept her. If they stay for Wave 3, the civilians are safe but the Threadcutter gets a head start.

**Quests**:
- Save Loom's population (or as many as possible)
- Decide: finish the defense or pursue the Threadcutter

**Rewards**:
- If Loom is saved: permanent safe haven, gratitude of the Remnants, Covenant reinforcements for the Pylon
- If Loom is abandoned: faster arrival at the Pylon, but guilt and loss of potential allies
- Either way: the party now knows where the Threadcutter is going

**Hooks**: The Eld Pylon of Keth lies a day's travel east. The Threadcutter is already en route. The clock is ticking.

# [Chapter 6: The Eld Pylon](chapter-06.md)
**Session 6-7 | Level 6**

**Full Detailed Breakdown**: See [chapter-06.md](chapter-06.md)

**Summary**: The party reaches the Eld Pylon of Keth — a massive crystalline tower rising from the wasteland, dormant but intact. Its interior is a vertical dungeon of ancient magitech: automated defenses, environmental puzzles, and the ghosts of the Old Ones' purpose encoded in its walls. At the control chamber, they encounter Commander Dace, the Threadcutter's pragmatic lieutenant. Dace can be fought, negotiated with, or turned — he believes in the cause but struggles with the methods. The Pylon's systems reveal a terrible truth: the Beams may be both sutures *and* chains. The Old Ones' purpose was more complex than either faction believed.

**Key Scenes**:
- Approach to the Pylon: the tower rises from shattered earth, crystal facets catching impossible light
- The Entry Hall: automated defense puzzle (magitech golems that test intent, not combat prowess)
- The Resonance Chamber: vertical climb through the Pylon's interior, environmental hazards
- The Control Chamber: confrontation with Commander Dace, the Old Ones' revelation
- Activating the Pylon's defenses in preparation for the Threadcutter's arrival

**NPCs:** [Commander Dace](npcs.md#commander-dace), [Moth](npcs.md#moth) (lore integration)

**Locations:** [The Eld Pylon of Keth](locations.md#the-eld-pylon-of-keth)

**Encounters**:
- **The Entry Hall** (puzzle): Two Pylon Guardians (Shield Guardians, MM p.271) block the entrance. They don't attack — they scan visitors. Only those touched by the Constant can pass. The party's bond is the key. Moth can help communicate with the Guardians if present.
- **The Resonance Chamber** (exploration/hazard): A vertical shaft lined with crystal nodes. The party must climb while avoiding resonance pulses (2d6 thunder damage, DC 14 Dexterity save) and activating crystalline platforms (DC 13 Arcana or Investigation). Moth can interface with the systems to reduce the DC.
- **Commander Dace** (social/combat): Dace occupies the Control Chamber with 3 Unraveler soldiers. He's studying the Pylon's systems, preparing for Sera's arrival. He can be convinced to stand down (DC 16 Persuasion, advantage if the party presents evidence of the Breaker Station's cruelty or the Old Ones' true purpose). If fought, he's a Warlord (VGM p.220) who fights tactically and surrenders at low HP rather than die for a cause he's questioning.
- **The Old Ones' Truth** (revelation): The Pylon's memory banks reveal that the Beams were designed as a *transitional* system — meant to stabilize reality during a period of planar upheaval, then be *gradually* dismantled as the world adapted. The Old Ones intended for the Beams to come down eventually. They just didn't intend for it to happen all at once.

**Quests**:
- Navigate the Pylon's interior
- Confront or turn Commander Dace
- Learn the truth about the Beams
- Prepare the Pylon's defenses for the Threadcutter

**Rewards**:
- Essence Pistol (rare weapon, found in the Pylon's armory)
- The Old Ones' truth (critical leverage for Chapter 7 negotiations)
- Commander Dace as a potential ally (if turned)
- Milestone: level up to 7 at chapter end

**Hooks**: The Threadcutter is hours away. The Pylon's defenses are active but incomplete. The party must decide how to use the time they have — and what to do with the knowledge that the Threadcutter's cause isn't entirely wrong.

# [Chapter 7: The Crimson Threshold](chapter-07.md)
**Session 7-8 | Level 7**

**Full Detailed Breakdown**: See [chapter-07.md](chapter-07.md)

**Summary**: Sera Mourne — the Threadcutter — arrives at the Eld Pylon of Keth with her remaining Unraveler forces and the Resonance Key, a device that can shatter the Pylon's crystal matrix and destroy the fifth Beam. The climax is a multi-phase encounter mixing combat with moral reckoning. Sera's argument has weight: the Old Ones' own records suggest the Beams were never meant to be permanent. But her methods — the Breaker Stations, the lives destroyed — undermine her cause. The party's choices throughout the campaign converge here.

**Key Scenes**:
- The Threadcutter's arrival: Sera approaches under a flag of parlay
- The Argument: Sera presents her case, referencing the Old Ones' records
- The Reckoning: combat erupts (or doesn't) based on the party's response
- The Beam's Fate: the final choice — preserve, destroy, or modify
- Epilogue: the aftermath and what comes next

**NPCs:** [Sera Mourne](npcs.md#sera-mourne-the-threadcutter), [Commander Dace](npcs.md#commander-dace), [Wren](npcs.md#wren) (if present), [Aldric Vane](npcs.md#aldric-vane) (via communication crystal)

**Locations:** [The Eld Pylon of Keth](locations.md#the-eld-pylon-of-keth)

**Encounters**:
- **The Parlay** (social): Sera enters under truce. She's calm, articulate, and genuinely believes she's saving the world. She presents the Old Ones' transition plan and argues that the Beams are overdue for removal. If the party has evidence from the Pylon's memory banks, they can counter her argument. This scene determines whether combat can be avoided entirely.
- **Phase 1 — The Assault** (combat): If negotiations fail, Sera activates the Resonance Key and her forces attack. 6 Unraveler soldiers, 2 Channelers. The Resonance Key begins a 10-round countdown to Beam destruction. The party must stop the Key or defeat Sera.
- **Phase 2 — The Surge** (combat/hazard): At round 5 (or when Sera drops below half HP), the Pylon's crystal matrix destabilizes. Reality warps: gravity shifts, time stutters, Far Realm energies bleed through. All combatants must make DC 14 Wisdom saves each round or suffer confusion (as the spell). The environment becomes the enemy.
- **Phase 3 — The Choice** (narrative/combat): Sera reaches the Pylon's core and places the Resonance Key. The party has one final chance: destroy the Key (combat), convince Sera to stop (DC 18 Persuasion, DC reduced by 2 for each major piece of evidence presented), or channel the Pylon's energy to create a third option (requires Wren, Moth, or a PC with the right skills and a willingness to take damage).

**Quests**:
- Confront the Threadcutter
- Decide the fate of the fifth Beam
- Resolve the campaign's central moral question

**Rewards**:
- Resonance Key (if preserved — extremely powerful plot item for future campaigns)
- Resolution and consequences based on choices
- If Sera survives: her knowledge becomes available (she's the foremost expert on the Beams)
- The party's reputation: they are now known to the Covenant, the Unravelers, and the Constant itself as people who shape the fate of reality

**Hooks**:
- The sixth Beam is still out there — and still threatened
- The original Eld Pylon, the Tower at the center of all things, awaits
- The Constant has noticed what the party did. New Doors are opening.
