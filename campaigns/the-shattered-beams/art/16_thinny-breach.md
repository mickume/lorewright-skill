A cracked stone courtyard at night, forty feet square, surrounded by a 
shimmering hemisphere of pale blue energy -- the Waystation's barrier. On 
the north wall, a ten-foot-wide crack runs through the barrier like a 
fracture in ice, its edges rippling and distorting. Through the crack, the 
Ashlands bleed in: colors run like wet paint at the edges, the ground 
beneath the breach writhes with wrongness. Two aberrant shapes have pushed 
through -- twisted geometry made flesh, eyes in impossible places, mouths 
that open in every direction. A hunched figure with a single burning eye 
crouches near rubble, watching with terrible intelligence. The contrast 
is stark: inside the barrier, the courtyard is stone and firelight and 
reality. At the breach, reality unravels. The pale blue barrier light 
flickers and stutters around the crack, fighting to hold. The squat 
watchtower rises behind to the south. Dramatic lighting -- the barrier's 
blue glow against the sickly iridescent wrongness pouring through the 
breach. Dark fantasy, painterly, atmospheric, urgent.
