A pool of still, clear water sits in a depression between two bone-white 
dead trees in the Ashlands. The sky above is amber-grey and bruised, but 
the water's reflection shows something entirely different: a deep night 
sky thick with unfamiliar constellations and a fat crescent moon. The 
reflected stars move at a different speed than the world above. A silver 
fish leaps in the reflected night sky -- caught mid-arc, trailing droplets 
that fall upward into the wrong sky. The pool's edges are ash-grey cracked 
earth, and faint crystalline fragments scattered nearby catch fragments 
of both skies -- the real and the reflected. Deep beneath the reflected 
water, two faint points of light like ancient eyes peer upward. The 
composition is split between the real world above and the impossible 
reflection below, creating a sense of dislocation and temporal wrongness. 
Muted earth tones for the real world, cool blues and silvers for the 
reflection. Dark fantasy exterior, painterly, atmospheric, eerie.
