The view from atop the darkened Eld Pylon, looking outward through broken 
crystal windows at a world unraveling. The Pylon is dark -- the crystal 
matrix has shattered, its remains littering the chamber floor like funeral 
confetti, each shard catching the last fading traces of the Beam's light. 
Beyond the windows, a wave of distortion ripples outward across the 
Ashlands. The sky tears -- dozens of wounds opening and closing like 
mouths gasping for air, each showing glimpses of impossible places: a sky 
with three suns, a forest of glass, an ocean that flows upward. Thinnies 
bloom across the landscape like poisonous flowers of light, tears in 
reality that will not close. The ground heaves. In the far distance, a 
city flickers through time periods so fast it becomes a strobe. The amber 
sky is three colors that should not coexist. The atmosphere is one of 
terrible consequence -- the cost of conviction written across the world 
in wounds of light. The world is dying faster now. Violent light against 
encroaching wrongness. Dark fantasy exterior, painterly, atmospheric, 
devastating.
