The tilted interior of a crashed skyship. The corridors are canted at 
thirty degrees, so adventurers walk on what was once the wall, with the 
actual floor rising beside them like a ramp. Doors hang open at wrong 
angles. Cargo has slid and settled against the lower walls. A single 
arcane lantern swings from a hook, still lit with a faint orange flame 
that has burned for months without fuel, casting warm light across the 
disorder. On the tilted bridge, a navigation table with pinned charts, 
a compass that still spins aimlessly, and a fist-sized crystal in a 
brass receptacle pulsing with faint amber light -- a log crystal holding 
the captain's final recording. Chalk marks on the corridor wall where 
someone measured the tilt every hour. A captain's coat hung neatly on 
a peg, as if she expected to come back for it. The atmosphere is one of 
slow, dignified catastrophe -- a ship that fell by degrees, its crew 
professional to the end. Warm lantern light against cold metal. Dark 
fantasy interior, painterly, atmospheric, melancholic.
