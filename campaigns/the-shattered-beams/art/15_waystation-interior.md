Interior of a circular stone room inside a crumbling watchtower. Low 
vaulted ceiling cracked with age, held together by roots that have grown 
through the masonry and chosen to grip rather than destroy. A fire burns 
in a central pit, casting warm orange light that plays against the pale 
blue glow filtering through windows and doorways from the barrier outside. 
Smoke rises through a chimney that was once an arrow slit. The walls are 
covered in scrawled notes, tacked-up maps with routes crossed out, and a 
calendar with far too many days marked off -- the accumulated evidence of 
decades of solitary vigil. Bedrolls line one wall, more than are needed. 
A table holds a hand-drawn map weighted with stones. A rack near the door 
holds supplies: water skins, a battered lantern, a crossbow gathering 
dust. The room feels like a campfire in the wilderness -- warm and safe, 
but the darkness is right there. Warm firelight mixed with the cool blue 
of the barrier's glow. Dark fantasy interior, painterly, atmospheric, 
intimate.
