Portrait of a young human woman, sixteen years old, gaunt and fragile 
but with fierce, luminous grey eyes that seem to look through things rather than 
at them. Shaved head, dark circles under her eyes, sharp cheekbones made sharper 
by weeks of captivity. She wears a shapeless grey shift, and faint marks from 
restraints are visible on her wrists. Despite her frailty, there is defiance in 
her posture — shoulders pulling back, chin lifting. A faint silver-white glow 
emanates from her eyes, barely perceptible, suggesting psychic power barely 
contained. The background is dark — the cold metal and violet crystal of the 
Breaker Station, with faint conduit lights creating geometric patterns behind 
her. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, the silver glow from her eyes the brightest 
point. Dark fantasy portrait, painterly, emotional, atmospheric.
