The hexagonal chamber of the Ruin of Vethara at the moment the security 
system triggers. The crystalline circuits in the dark metal walls have 
shifted from amber to pulsing red, bathing the room in alarm light. Two 
sealed alcoves in the walls crack open, dark metal grinding apart, and 
from within, two seven-foot humanoid constructs step forward -- encased 
in dark metal armor etched with triangular geometric patterns, their 
helm-slits glowing red, their hands ending in bladed gauntlets that hum 
with contained force. From a rear passage, a third, larger construct 
emerges -- more ornate, its helm crowned with crystalline spines, 
translucent crystalline wings beginning to unfold from its back. It 
positions itself protectively before a sealed alcove in the far wall. At 
the chamber's center, Moth -- a warforged of tarnished bronze with one 
amber eye -- presses itself back against the crystal plinth, alarmed. 
The six crystal nodes at the ceiling pulse red in rhythm with the alarm 
tone. The scene captures the instant ancient security protocols wake to 
confront intruders. Red alarm light clashing with fading amber. Dark 
fantasy interior, painterly, atmospheric, threatening.
