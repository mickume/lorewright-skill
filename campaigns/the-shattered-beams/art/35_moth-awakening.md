A hexagonal chamber thirty feet across inside an Old Ones facility. The 
ceiling rises to a point where six crystal nodes converge, emitting a 
soft, steady amber glow that fills the room without shadows. On a plinth 
of pale crystal at the chamber's center, a warforged construct sits 
upright -- medium build, tarnished bronze and pale crystal, moss and 
lichen cracking away from its joints as it moves for the first time in 
centuries. One eye -- an amber crystal lens -- glows bright with renewed 
awareness. The other eye is dark, its crystal cracked with a spiderweb 
of fracture lines. The construct's fingers are mid-twitch, the mechanical 
equivalent of a sleeper waking. Green fragments of moss fall from its 
shoulders. The chamber's walls display fractal crystalline circuitry 
pulsing with amber light. Six alcoves line the hexagonal walls, each 
containing alien equipment. The atmosphere is one of ancient wonder and 
gentle awakening -- something old remembering what it is. Warm amber 
light throughout. Dark fantasy interior, painterly, atmospheric, reverent.
