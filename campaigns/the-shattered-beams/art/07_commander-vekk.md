Portrait of a female dwarf with a shaved head and a prominent scar running 
from her left temple to her jaw -- old, well-healed, the kind earned in a 
fight she won. Stocky and solid, she radiates the no-nonsense authority of 
a career soldier. Her eyes are grey-green and flat, the eyes of someone 
who has learned not to think too hard about the orders she follows. She 
wears practical Unraveler armor -- dark leather and metal plate with the 
broken circle insignia, dented and scratched from use. A warhammer with 
a faintly luminous crystalline head rests against her shoulder, held 
casually in one hand. Behind her, the utilitarian corridors of the Breaker 
Station -- dark Old Ones alloy repurposed with rough military furniture, 
torchlight throwing uneven shadows, supply crates stamped with Unraveler 
marks. Her expression is not cruel -- it is efficient. The face of a 
pragmatist who does what needs to be done and does not ask if it should be. 
Warm torchlight from the left, violet magitech glow from conduits on the 
right. Dark fantasy portrait, painterly, atmospheric.
