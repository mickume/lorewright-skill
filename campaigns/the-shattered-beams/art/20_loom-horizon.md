A city on the horizon that flickers between three versions of itself, 
seen from a distance across the Ashlands. The settlement phases in layers 
like a triple-exposed photograph: gleaming crystalline Old Ones towers 
rising impossibly tall, medieval stone walls and thatched roofs, and 
crumbling ruins overgrown with dead vegetation. The three versions shimmer 
and blend at the edges, translucent and ghostly, none fully solid. At the 
city's center, one building glows with steady warm light -- the anchor, 
the only stable point. The foreground is Ashlands: cracked ash-grey earth, 
a pale road stretching toward the city, dead trees standing like bones. 
The sky above the city phases too -- patches of blue, amber, and starless 
black overlapping in impossible layers. The city is beautiful and 
heartbreaking -- a place that cannot decide if it exists. Wide establishing 
shot from the travelers' perspective, looking across the wasteland toward 
the flickering settlement. Dark fantasy, painterly, atmospheric, surreal.
