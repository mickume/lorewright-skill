A vast interior chamber inside the Eld Pylon. The ceiling arches sixty 
feet overhead, supported by pillars of translucent crystal that glow with 
a soft, steady light -- no torches, no flame, only the ambient luminescence 
of Old Ones engineering. The floor is a mosaic of geometric patterns in 
dark stone and pale crystal, radiating outward from the center in concentric 
rings -- too precise to be decorative, feeling functional, like circuitry 
written in architecture. Two crystalline figures stand at the chamber's 
center, flanking the path forward: 10 feet tall, humanoid, made of the 
same faceted crystal as the Pylon itself. Their heads are featureless 
except for a single horizontal slit from which beams of pale blue light 
sweep the chamber in slow, methodical arcs. The Guardians are still, 
patient, ancient -- security protocols that have been waiting millennia 
for someone to walk through the door. The scale is awe-inspiring. The 
architecture feels simultaneously technological and sacred. Cool 
crystalline lighting, geometric shadows. Dark fantasy interior, painterly, 
atmospheric, reverent.
