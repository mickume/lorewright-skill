A street inside a city caught in temporal flux, seen from ground level as 
if walking through it for the first time. The buildings on either side 
shimmer between eras: a stone wall with moss becomes crystalline Old Ones 
architecture for a heartbeat, then crumbling ruins, then stone again. The 
cobblestones underfoot are half-medieval, half-geometric crystal paving, 
the seam between them rippling like water. People walk the center of the 
street with practiced caution, avoiding walls that might phase -- their 
postures alert, habitual, resigned. A child stands on cobblestones that 
shimmer beneath their feet. In the distance, the Council Hall radiates 
steady warm light, the one structure that doesn't flicker. The edges of 
every surface glow faintly with temporal bleed -- watermark ghosts of other 
eras visible like double images. The air carries visible distortion: faint 
heat-haze shimmer between the buildings. Street-level perspective, 
immersive. Dark fantasy, painterly, atmospheric, surreal.
