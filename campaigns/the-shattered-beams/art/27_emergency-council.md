Interior of the Council Hall during a crisis. A modest stone chamber with 
thick walls laced with veins of crystal that pulse with a slow, steady 
Beam-shard glow -- the only stable place in a city tearing itself apart. 
A central table of dark wood holds a map of Loom, weighted at the corners 
with chunks of stabilized crystal. Frightened families press against the 
walls, clutching children and belongings. Militia members stand in uncertain 
knots, gripping weapons. A woman with silver-streaked black hair -- Elder 
Soraya -- stands at the head of the table, hands flat on its surface, jaw 
set, eyes burning with the resolve of a leader who knows she is out of her 
depth but will not show it. The warm amber light from the Beam-shard 
fragments contrasts with the chaotic temporal shimmer visible through 
windows -- outside, buildings flicker and phase violently. The air inside 
is still and stable while the world beyond the walls is coming apart. 
Warm interior lighting against cold exterior chaos. Dark fantasy interior, 
painterly, atmospheric, tense.
