A chamber within the Eld Pylon where crystal walls play holographic 
recordings. The walls are floor-to-ceiling panels of pale, translucent 
crystal, and from their surfaces, ghostly holographic images project into 
the room: geometric diagrams of the Ley Beam network, stylized figures 
of the Old Ones (tall, angular, barely humanoid silhouettes rendered in 
light), and sweeping vistas of a world that no longer exists -- green 
forests, intact cities, a sky that is properly blue. The holograms overlap 
and layer, creating a dense tapestry of light and memory that fills the 
chamber with shifting illumination. A figure (or figures) stand among the 
projections, dwarfed by the scale of the recordings, reaching toward an 
image that shows six lines of light extending from a crystalline tower -- 
the Ley Beams as they were meant to be. The floor's geometric patterns 
glow in response to the projections. The atmosphere is one of revelation 
and loss -- the memory of a civilization that built something magnificent 
and never finished it. Cool blue and warm amber holographic light. Dark 
fantasy interior, painterly, atmospheric, melancholic.
