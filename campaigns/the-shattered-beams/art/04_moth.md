Portrait of a warforged construct of tarnished bronze and pale crystal. Medium 
humanoid build, but clearly mechanical — plates of darkened metal, joints filled 
with moss and lichen from centuries of dormancy. One eye glows faintly amber, 
warm and curious. The other eye is dark and cracked, the crystal lens fractured. 
Geometric patterns are etched into the chest and face plates — Old Ones' script 
in precise, mathematical lines. The surface is pitted with age, tarnished green 
in places, but the underlying craftsmanship is elegant. The warforged's head is 
tilted at a precise angle, as if processing something. Behind them, the dark 
metal walls of an ancient ruin, geometric and alien. Warm amber lighting from 
the functional eye contrasts with cool shadows. Dark fantasy portrait, 
painterly, atmospheric, melancholic.
