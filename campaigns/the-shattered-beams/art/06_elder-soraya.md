Portrait of a human woman in her mid-fifties with sharp dark eyes that 
calculate cost and benefit in real time. Silver-streaked black hair pulled 
back tight against her skull, emphasizing high cheekbones and a jaw set 
with exhausted determination. She wears practical clothes -- a leather jerkin 
over a linen shirt, no finery, no affectation. One piece of jewelry: a 
pendant on a thin silver chain, a shard of Beam crystal that pulses with 
a slow, rhythmic inner light. Her hands rest on the edge of a dark wooden 
table scarred with use, maps and supply lists visible beneath her fingers. 
Behind her, the warm steady glow of the Council Hall's Beam-shard-stabilized 
walls -- the one still point in a city that phases between eras. Her 
expression is the look of a woman who has kept 800 people alive through 
impossible compromises and is running out of compromises to make. Warm 
amber lighting from the Beam-shard fragments in the walls, contrasting 
with the faint temporal shimmer visible through a window behind her. Dark 
fantasy portrait, painterly, atmospheric.
