Interior of a narrow, cluttered apothecary shop in a side street. Shelves 
line every wall from floor to ceiling, crammed with glass jars of dried 
herbs, ceramic pots of tinctures, bundles of dried flowers, and dubious 
remedies in unlabeled bottles. The ceiling is low, hung with drying plants 
that brush the head of anyone tall enough to notice. A wooden counter runs 
across the middle, its surface stained with years of spilled compounds. 
Behind the counter, a half-open door hints at a back room. The light comes 
from a single oil lamp and the faint temporal shimmer bleeding through the 
shop's one small window -- the green-tinted glass distorts the phasing 
street outside into abstract color. The shop smells of dried rosemary and 
something metallic. A trapdoor in the floor is barely visible beneath a 
threadbare rug -- the entrance to the Covenant's hidden cell below. The 
atmosphere is cluttered, secretive, lived-in. Warm lamplight against cool 
exterior shimmer. Dark fantasy interior, painterly, atmospheric, intimate.
