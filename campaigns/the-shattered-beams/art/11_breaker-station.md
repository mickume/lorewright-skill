The interior of an underground facility: the Breaker Chamber. A circular room 
of dark metal and crystalline conduits, lit by sickly violet light that pulses 
in rhythmic patterns. Eight magitech chairs are arranged in a circle around a 
central crystal node that glows with concentrated energy. Five of the chairs 
are occupied by gaunt, semiconscious figures — their heads connected to the 
chairs by crystalline tendrils at the temples, their eyes open but unseeing, 
their bodies wasted. The conduits running from the chairs pulse with their 
channeled energy, carrying it upward through the ceiling. The air seems to 
shimmer with psychic distortion. The room is cold and clinical, Old Ones 
architecture repurposed with brutal efficiency — welded plates and jury-rigged 
modifications overlaying elegant geometric design. The central crystal node 
casts harsh violet shadows across the occupants' faces. Claustrophobic, 
oppressive, horrifying. Dark fantasy, painterly, atmospheric.
