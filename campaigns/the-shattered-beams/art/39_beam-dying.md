A vertical composition inside the Control Chamber of the Eld Pylon. The 
crystal matrix has fractured, pieces falling away like shards of a broken 
mirror, each fragment trailing threads of solidified light that snap and 
dissolve. Through the matrix's skeleton, the Beam itself is visible -- a 
vast pillar of light shooting upward through the crystal and into the sky, 
wavering like a candle flame in a hurricane. The light is achingly 
beautiful -- the color of every dawn compressed into a single column, 
ancient and luminous and dying. Crystal shards float and fall around the 
Beam, each catching its light and scattering prismatic fragments. The 
floor below is littered with fallen crystal, the geometric inscriptions 
still glowing. Far above, the Beam stretches toward a sky that has 
forgotten how to be blue. The composition is vertical, looking upward 
through the dying matrix at the exposed Beam -- a pillar of light that 
is the most beautiful and terrible thing in the world. Prismatic light 
against darkness. Dark fantasy interior, painterly, atmospheric, 
heartbreaking.
