A city in temporal flux, seen from a slight elevation. The settlement is layered 
with three overlapping eras visible simultaneously, translucent and ghostly: 
medieval stone buildings with thatched roofs, gleaming crystalline Old Ones 
architecture rising impossibly tall, and crumbling ruins overgrown with 
vegetation. The three versions shimmer and phase, blending at the edges like a 
triple-exposed photograph. At the city's center, a stone council hall glows with 
steady Beam-shard light — the one stable anchor in the chaos. People walk the 
streets, their forms solid against the phasing architecture, moving with the 
practiced caution of those who've learned to navigate impossible geography. 
An open market in the foreground shows merchants from different eras side by 
side, unaware of each other. The sky above phases too — patches of blue, amber, 
and starless black overlapping. Atmospheric, surreal, painterly, dark fantasy.
