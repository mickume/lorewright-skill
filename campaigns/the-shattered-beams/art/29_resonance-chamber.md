A vertical shaft inside the Eld Pylon, one hundred feet tall, lined with 
crystal nodes that glow in shifting patterns of blue and amber. The shaft 
is viewed from within, looking upward -- a dizzying perspective of 
concentric crystal rings receding toward a distant point of light far 
above. Platforms of solid light -- translucent, geometric, glowing pale 
blue -- extend from the walls at irregular intervals, some fully formed, 
others flickering in and out of existence. The crystal nodes pulse in 
waves, sending resonance patterns cascading up and down the shaft like 
a visual heartbeat. The architecture is the Old Ones at their most 
vertiginous: impossible geometry, surfaces that seem to shift when observed 
directly, light that bends around corners. The scale is immense and 
disorienting. Faint geometric dust motes float in the shaft, caught in 
the crystal light. The atmosphere is one of ascending through the inside 
of a living instrument. Vertical composition, looking upward. Cool 
crystalline blues and ambers. Dark fantasy interior, painterly, 
atmospheric, awe-inspiring.
