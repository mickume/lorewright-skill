An open-air marketplace where merchants from different time periods sell 
goods simultaneously, unaware of each other. A present-day baker tends a 
stone oven beside a ghostly, translucent Old Ones vendor whose crystalline 
stall displays geometric tools that hum with dormant energy. Further along, 
a future-era stall flickers between existence and ruin -- mostly empty 
wreckage, occasionally solidifying into something strange and unfamiliar. 
The market stalls are a patchwork of materials: rough-hewn wood, phased-in 
crystal surfaces, crumbling stone. Shoppers move between the vendors with 
the practiced nonchalance of people accustomed to the impossible -- stepping 
around gaps in reality, reaching for goods that might or might not be there. 
The ground phases between cobblestone and geometric crystal paving. 
Overhead, the sky is visible in patches of conflicting color -- amber, blue, 
and starless black. Warm market light mixed with the cool glow of phased-in 
magitech. Dark fantasy, painterly, atmospheric, surreal, lived-in.
