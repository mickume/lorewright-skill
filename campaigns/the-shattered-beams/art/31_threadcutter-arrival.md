A column of dark figures approaches the base of the Eld Pylon of Keth, 
seen from above -- from the Control Chamber's crystal windows, sixty feet 
up. The massive crystalline tower dominates the right side of the frame, 
its faceted surface scattering prismatic light across the ash-grey 
wasteland. Below, a woman walks at the head of the column with the 
unhurried certainty of someone who believes history is on her side. She 
is lean, dark-haired, wearing a scholar's coat over traveling leathers. 
In her right hand, a geometric crystalline device -- the Resonance Key -- 
catches the light with an unnatural intensity. Behind her, six soldiers in 
dark armor with broken-circle insignia march in military formation, flanked 
by two robed figures. The amber sky stretches above, bruised and heavy. 
The Pylon's prismatic light dims subtly as the column approaches -- the 
crystal matrix reacting to the Key's presence. The scene feels like the 
final act beginning: inevitable, measured, terrible. High-angle shot 
looking down from the Pylon. Dark fantasy, painterly, atmospheric, 
cinematic.
