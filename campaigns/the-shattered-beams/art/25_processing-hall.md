Interior of an underground facility: Level 2 of the Breaker Station. A 
long chamber of dark Old Ones alloy, repurposed with brutal efficiency. 
Crystalline conduits run along the walls and ceiling, pulsing with sickly 
violet light in rhythmic patterns -- the visual heartbeat of the machinery. 
A control station of jury-rigged consoles and salvaged Old Ones interfaces 
sits at one end, its surfaces cluttered with tools and handwritten notes. 
The conduits converge from the ceiling above and descend through the floor 
below, connecting the Breaker Chamber beneath to the broadcast antenna 
above. Welded metal plates and improvised modifications overlay the 
elegant geometric design of the original architecture -- a crude human 
layer forced onto something alien and precise. Bioluminescent crystal 
strips provide dim ambient light. The air shimmers faintly with psychic 
distortion. The room feels oppressive, warm, and wrong -- a machine that 
does something terrible, maintained with clinical efficiency. Violet 
magitech lighting dominates, with harsh shadows. Dark fantasy interior, 
painterly, atmospheric, claustrophobic.
