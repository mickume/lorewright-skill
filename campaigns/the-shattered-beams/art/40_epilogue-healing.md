The view from atop the Eld Pylon, sixty feet above the Ashlands, looking 
outward through shattered crystal windows. The Beam's light has 
strengthened -- warm and steady, casting prismatic patterns across a 
landscape that is beginning to heal. At the Pylon's base, the ash-grey 
earth is darkening, enriching, becoming soil. Blades of grass push 
through -- small, tentative, but unmistakably alive. Tendrils of moss 
creep across crystalline ruins. The sky is clearing to a blue deeper and 
broader than any natural sky, with room for colors that did not exist 
before. In the distance, a Thinny that was blooming on the horizon 
contracts, fades, dissolves -- the boundary between worlds adjusting, 
healing. The Pylon's crystal matrix is visible at the frame's edge, 
cracked and scarred but whole, its fractures filled with new light 
brighter than before. Wind stirs the first new growth. The atmosphere is 
one of fragile, hard-won hope -- a broken world daring to remember what 
life feels like. Warm light, new greens against grey earth, clearing sky. 
Dark fantasy exterior, painterly, atmospheric, hopeful.
