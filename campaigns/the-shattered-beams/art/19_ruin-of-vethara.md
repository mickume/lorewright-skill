A half-buried Old Ones facility emerging from a geometric hillside. A 
triangular door of dark metal is set flush into the earth, its surface 
inscribed with precise crystalline circuitry that is dormant -- dark 
lines tracing mathematical patterns across the door's face. The hillside 
around it is unnaturally angular, as if the earth was shaped rather than 
eroded, with faceted planes of compacted soil and exposed dark alloy 
beneath. Pale crystal fragments are embedded in the surrounding rock, 
their light extinguished. Moss and lichen creep across the metal surfaces, 
centuries of nature reclaiming what was built to outlast it. The entrance 
is partially obscured by a fall of loose earth and dead vegetation. The 
air around the door seems cleaner, sharper -- a pocket of stability in the 
blighted landscape. The Ashlands stretch behind the viewer: ash-grey earth, 
dead bone-white trees, bruised sky. The ruin feels ancient, patient, and 
waiting. Dramatic natural light from the amber sky, with faint residual 
glow from the deepest crystal fragments. Dark fantasy, painterly, 
atmospheric.
