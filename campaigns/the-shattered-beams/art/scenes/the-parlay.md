The Control Chamber of the Eld Pylon, sixty feet above the Ashlands. A 
lean, dark-haired woman in a scholar's coat, Sera Mourne, over traveling leathers stands 
three paces inside the doorway, her ink-stained hands folded in front of 
her. She has set a geometric crystalline device -- the Resonance Key -- 
on the nearest console, a deliberate gesture of disarmament. Her 
expression is calm, certain, respectful -- the face of someone presenting 
a thesis, not making a threat. Above her, the crystal matrix hangs from 
the ceiling like a chandelier carved from a single impossible gem, fifteen 
feet across, pulsing with deep rhythmic light. Control consoles ring the 
circular walls. Windows of transparent crystal show the amber Ashlands 
stretching to the horizon. The Beam's light filters through the matrix, 
casting prismatic patterns across the chamber. The scene is a moment of 
profound tension -- a woman who has walked through hell to reach this 
room, standing before the thing she came to destroy, choosing to talk 
first. Cool crystalline light, warm amber accents. Dark fantasy interior, 
painterly, atmospheric, tense.
