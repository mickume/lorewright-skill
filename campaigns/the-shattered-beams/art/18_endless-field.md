A flat, featureless plain under a bruised amber-grey sky where ghostly 
soldiers are locked in an endlessly repeating battle. The translucent 
figures -- dozens of them -- clash with spectral weapons, their forms 
flickering and resetting like a scratched record. Some are mid-swing, 
frozen between moments. Others dissolve into particles of light and 
reform in their starting positions, doomed to fight and die and fight 
again. The soldiers wear armor from a forgotten era, their banners 
faded to illegibility. The ground beneath them is ash-grey and unmarked 
-- no blood, no footprints, no evidence that any of this is physically 
real. At the edges of the field, the temporal loop's boundary is visible 
as a faint shimmer in the air, like heat haze. Beyond it, the normal 
Ashlands continue. The light from the ghostly combatants casts a pale, 
cold glow across the plain. The scene is beautiful and deeply wrong -- 
a war that will never end because time has forgotten how to let it. 
Wide panoramic shot. Dark fantasy, painterly, atmospheric, haunting.
