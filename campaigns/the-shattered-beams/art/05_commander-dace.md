Portrait of a human man in his late thirties with close-cropped brown hair 
and a military bearing that contrasts with tired, uncertain eyes. His jaw 
is set rigid but his gaze carries the weight of doubt he hasn't voiced. He 
wears practical plate-and-leather armor with the Unraveler insignia -- a 
broken circle -- stamped into the shoulder guard. The armor is well-maintained, 
functional, without ornament. A longsword hangs at his hip, its pommel 
worn smooth from habitual touch. His left hand rests on the pommel in a 
grounding gesture. Behind him, the geometric dark-metal walls and crystalline 
conduits of the Eld Pylon's Control Chamber, lit by the deep rhythmic glow 
of the Beam anchor crystal above. His expression is that of a soldier who 
followed orders because he trusted the cause, and is realizing, too late, 
that trust is not the same as certainty. Dramatic lighting from the crystal's 
glow above, casting amber highlights against violet shadows from the Pylon's 
systems. Dark fantasy portrait, painterly, atmospheric.
