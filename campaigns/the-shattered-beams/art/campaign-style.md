Dark fantasy illustration in a painterly, atmospheric style. The aesthetic blends 
post-apocalyptic desolation with crystalline, otherworldly beauty — a dying world 
that is still hauntingly magnificent.

Color palette: muted earth tones (ash grey, burnt umber, faded amber) punctuated 
by vivid accents of pale blue (Beam energy), deep violet (Breaker/Unraveler 
technology), and prismatic crystalline highlights. Skies should be bruised — 
amber-grey with wrong-colored light.

Lighting: dramatic chiaroscuro with an emphasis on light sources that feel 
unnatural or otherworldly — crystalline glows, Beam energy, the shimmer of 
reality tearing. Natural light (sun, fire) should feel slightly off: too warm, 
too cold, or casting shadows at wrong angles.

Architecture: the Old Ones' technology should feel alien and geometric — 
crystalline surfaces, dark metal, patterns that suggest mathematics rather than 
decoration. Not steampunk. Think crystalline cathedrals designed by a 
civilization that saw no distinction between engineering and prayer.

Characters: realistic proportions, weathered and worn. Clothing is practical, 
layered, patched. Military gear is functional, not ornamental. Expressions 
should carry weight — exhaustion, determination, quiet grief, cautious hope.

Atmosphere: every image should feel inhabited by the world's wrongness. Subtle 
details — shadows falling at impossible angles, a flower growing through crystal, 
distant shapes that don't obey geometry — reinforce that this is a world where 
reality itself is fraying.

No anime, cartoon, or stylized proportions. No clean or pristine surfaces. 
Everything shows wear, age, and the passage of time in a world that has moved on.
