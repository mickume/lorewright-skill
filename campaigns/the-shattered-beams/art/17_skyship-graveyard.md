Three crashed skyships in a shallow valley of ash-grey earth under a 
bruised amber sky. The largest vessel is tilted at thirty degrees, its 
hull cracked open like an egg, revealing decayed decks and corridors 
within. Beneath its keel, a dead crystal engine -- dark, massive, the 
size of a horse -- hangs from corroded mountings. The second ship lies 
inverted, its mast-like structures driven into the ground. The third is 
barely recognizable -- scattered debris across the valley floor, only its 
skeletal frame suggesting it was once airborne. Crystalline fragments from 
the shattered engines catch the wrong-colored light and scatter fractured 
rainbows across the ash. A faint temporal shimmer surrounds the largest 
ship -- some sections preserved in stasis, others rotting, the contrast 
jarring and strange. Dead trees stand like bleached bones at the valley's 
edge. The scene is a monument to a world that fell out of the sky. Wide 
establishing shot, melancholic, haunting. Dark fantasy, painterly, 
atmospheric.
