Portrait of a lean, sharp-featured human woman in her early forties. Dark hair 
cut short and practical, framing a face of angles — high cheekbones, narrow jaw, 
deep-set eyes the color of burnt amber that burn with certainty. She wears a 
long coat of dark grey leather over practical armor, the Unraveler symbol 
(a broken circle) subtly embossed on the shoulder. At her hip, a crystalline 
device hangs from a chain — geometric, faceted, humming with faint violet light. 
Her expression is composed and resolute, carrying the weight of terrible 
decisions made with clear-eyed conviction. There is nothing soft about her, but 
there is nothing cruel either. Behind her, a fractured sky and the distant 
silhouette of a crystalline tower. Lighting from below, violet-tinged, casting 
dramatic shadows upward. Dark fantasy portrait, painterly, atmospheric.
