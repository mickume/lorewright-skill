A vast, blighted landscape stretching to the horizon under a bruised amber-grey 
sky. The terrain is cracked, ash-grey earth broken by dead trees that stand like 
bleached bones. Scattered across the landscape: crystalline fragments from 
shattered magitech, catching impossible light. A road cuts through the 
desolation but ends abruptly — beyond it, the terrain simply isn't there, 
replaced by a shimmering void. In the middle distance, the wreckage of a 
crashed skyship lies tilted in a shallow valley, its dead crystal engine dark 
against the sky. A pond in the foreground reflects a different sky than the one 
above — a blue sky with white clouds that doesn't match the amber reality. 
Shadows fall at subtly wrong angles. The scene is beautiful in its desolation: 
haunting, melancholic, wrong. Wide panoramic shot, dark fantasy, painterly, 
atmospheric.
