A massive crystalline tower rising 200 feet from shattered, uplifted earth 
against a fractured sky. The Eld Pylon of Keth: its surface is faceted like a 
cut gem, each facet catching light in prismatic patterns that scatter rainbows 
across the surrounding wasteland. The crystal is pale, almost translucent, with 
veins of deeper color — blue and violet — running through it like a living 
nervous system. The earth around the base is cracked and thrust upward, as if 
the tower punched through from deep underground. Crystalline fragments and 
debris scatter the ground, some still faintly glowing. The sky behind the Pylon 
is fractured — cracks of light run through the bruised amber, as if the tower 
is the only thing holding the sky together. A faint pillar of light — the Beam 
itself — rises from the tower's apex into the heavens, wavering like a candle 
flame in wind. Awe-inspiring, ancient, alien. Wide establishing shot, looking 
slightly upward. Dark fantasy, painterly, atmospheric.
