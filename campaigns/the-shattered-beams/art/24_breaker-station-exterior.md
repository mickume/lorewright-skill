A low, unremarkable hillside in barren terrain -- grey earth scrubbed clean 
of vegetation in a wide ring, no birds, no insects, oppressive silence 
made visible. The sky is the Ashlands' bruised amber-grey. Sparse lichen 
clings to weathered stone. A scree slope on the eastern face. Half-hidden 
behind a fall of loose rock, a reinforced metal door is set flush into the 
hillside -- Old Ones alloy, dark and seamless, with Unraveler sigils 
scratched into the frame in bright recent marks against ancient dark metal. 
The door is the only sign that something lies within. The terrain around it 
feels wrong -- too still, too clean, as if something beneath the hill has 
scoured the life from the surrounding earth. A faint haze of heat rises 
from the hillside, and the air carries the taste of copper and static. 
The isolation is total -- no roads, no landmarks, just the dead zone and 
the hidden door. Wide establishing shot, ominous, desolate. Dark fantasy 
landscape, painterly, atmospheric.
