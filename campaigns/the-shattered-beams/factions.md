# Factions & Organizations - The Shattered Beams

This document details the major groups, organizations, and factions in the campaign.

---

## Major Factions

### The Covenant of the Pylon

**Type:** Secret Society / Scholarly Order
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
**Symbol:** A vertical beam of light passing through a crystalline prism
**Motto:** *"The Pattern holds."*

#### Overview

The Covenant of the Pylon is the last organized group dedicated to maintaining the Ley Beams. Founded in the decades after the Fracturing by scholars who discovered the Eld Pylons and understood their purpose, the Covenant has spent two centuries studying, protecting, and — where possible — repairing the Beam network. They are the world's foremost experts on Old Ones technology, though their understanding is incomplete.

At its height, the Covenant numbered in the hundreds, with cells in every major settlement and agents stationed at each of the six Pylons. Now, with four Beams fallen and the world crumbling, the Covenant is a shadow of itself. Fewer than fifty active members remain, scattered across a handful of cells. Their knowledge is vast but their ability to act is crippled.

#### History & Origins

The Covenant was founded 180 years ago by a group of scholars who stumbled upon a functioning Eld Pylon and decoded enough of the Old Ones' records to understand the Beams' purpose. They organized in secret, recognizing that the Beams' existence was too important — and too easily exploited — to be public knowledge.

The Covenant's greatest crisis came five years ago when one of their most gifted scholars, [Sera Mourne](npcs.md#sera-mourne-the-threadcutter), discovered records suggesting the Beams were transitional. The Covenant's leadership suppressed her findings, fearing destabilization. Sera left and founded the Unravelers. The Covenant considers this their greatest institutional failure, though they refuse to admit it publicly.

#### Goals & Motivations

**Public Goals:** Maintain the Beams and preserve reality's stability.
**True Goals:** The same — but some members quietly wonder if Sera was right about the Beams' transitional nature. The leadership's orthodoxy is increasingly fragile.
**Methods:** Research, maintenance, and — when necessary — sabotage of threats to the Beams. They prefer knowledge to violence, but they've learned to fight.

#### Structure & Hierarchy

**Leadership:**
- **The Primer:** The Covenant's nominal leader. Currently vacant — the last Primer died two years ago and no successor has been chosen.
- **Keepers:** Senior members who run individual cells. Each Keeper operates semi-independently.

```
The Primer (vacant)
    ├── Keeper Jorin (Loom cell)
    ├── Keeper Aldric Vane (exiled — Waystation)
    ├── [2-3 other Keepers in distant cells]
    └── Agents and scholars (5-10 per cell)
```

**Membership:**
- **Size:** ~50 active members across the remaining world
- **Requirements:** Knowledge of the Beams, willingness to operate in secret, demonstrated loyalty
- **Ranks:** Initiate → Scholar → Keeper → Primer

#### Key NPCs

| Name | Rank/Role | Loyalty | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------|-------|
| [Aldric Vane](npcs.md#aldric-vane) | Exiled Keeper | Loyal to the cause, not the institution | Maintains the Waystation independently |
| Keeper Jorin | Active Keeper | Devoted but overwhelmed | Runs the Loom cell alone |

#### Resources & Assets

**Wealth:** Poor (their resources have dwindled with the world)
**Territory:** No territory — cells operate covertly within existing settlements
**Assets:**
- Deep knowledge of Old Ones technology and the Beam network
- Communication crystals linking cells across the continent
- A handful of magitech artifacts and tools
- The goodwill of those who understand what the Beams do

#### Relationship with the Party

**Initial Stance:** Cautiously hopeful. The Covenant has been waiting for the Constant to Draw people — it's mentioned in the Old Ones' records as a failsafe.

**How to Gain Favor:**
- Demonstrate understanding of the Beams
- Disrupt Unraveler operations
- Protect Beam-sensitive sites

**How to Earn Enmity:**
- Destroy or damage a Beam
- Ally openly with the Unravelers
- Treat the Beams as tools rather than sacred responsibilities

**Benefits of Alliance:**
- Knowledge of Old Ones technology and the Beam network
- Magitech supplies and equipment
- Safe houses in Covenant cells
- Intelligence on Unraveler operations

#### Secrets & Intrigue

**Public Doesn't Know:**
- The Covenant exists — most people have never heard of the Beams, let alone their guardians
- The Covenant suppressed Sera Mourne's research about the Beams' transitional nature

**Internal Conflicts:**
- Some members sympathize with Sera's findings and question whether the Covenant's orthodoxy is killing the world it claims to protect
- The lack of a Primer has created a leadership vacuum. Cells operate independently, sometimes at cross purposes.

### The Unravelers

**Type:** Revolutionary Organization
**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
**Symbol:** A broken circle — a ring with a gap, representing the severing of the Beams
**Motto:** *"The cage has a key."*

#### Overview

The Unravelers are a radical faction dedicated to destroying the Ley Beams and freeing reality from what they see as the Old Ones' imposed framework. Founded five years ago by [Sera Mourne](npcs.md#sera-mourne-the-threadcutter), they believe the Beams are chains, not sutures — a system of control that prevents the world from evolving beyond the Old Ones' design.

The Unravelers are not cartoonishly evil. Their members genuinely believe they're saving the world. Many joined after losing homes, families, or entire communities to the Beams' deterioration — they see the Beams as a failing system that should be dismantled before its uncontrolled collapse causes worse damage. Sera's argument — that the Old Ones designed the Beams as transitional and they're long overdue for removal — is compelling, even if her methods are monstrous.

#### History & Origins

Sera Mourne left the Covenant five years ago after her research was suppressed. She recruited followers from the disillusioned, the desperate, and the intellectually honest — people who looked at a world of crashed skyships, phasing cities, and reality storms and concluded that the system was already failing. The Unravelers grew quickly in a world where the status quo meant slow death.

The Breaker Stations were Sera's most controversial decision. Unable to fully reconstruct the Old Ones' controlled transition protocols, she devised an alternative: using captive psychics' energy to create interference patterns that erode the Beams. The method works. It is also, by any moral standard, horrific.

#### Goals & Motivations

**Public Goals:** Free reality from the Beams and allow the world to evolve naturally.
**True Goals:** The same — but the leadership is increasingly divided on the Breaker Stations. Sera insists they're necessary. [Commander Dace](npcs.md#commander-dace) and others have never visited one by deliberate choice.
**Methods:** Breaker Stations (psychic exploitation), the Resonance Key (direct Pylon destruction), sabotage of Covenant operations, and recruitment through ideological persuasion.

#### Structure & Hierarchy

**Leadership:**
- **The Threadcutter (Sera Mourne):** Visionary leader and sole authority on Beam technology
- **Commander Dace:** Military leader, handles logistics and operations

```
The Threadcutter (Sera Mourne)
    ├── Commander Dace (military operations)
    │   ├── Station Commanders (Vekk and others)
    │   │   └── Soldiers and Channelers
    │   └── Field operatives
    └── Research scholars (2-3 former Covenant members)
```

**Membership:**
- **Size:** ~80 members (30 soldiers, 10 Channelers, rest are support)
- **Requirements:** Belief in the cause, willingness to act on it
- **Ranks:** Operative → Channeler/Soldier → Station Commander → Commander

#### Key NPCs

| Name | Rank/Role | Loyalty | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------|-------|
| [Sera Mourne](npcs.md#sera-mourne-the-threadcutter) | Leader | Absolute — to the cause, not to people | The Threadcutter |
| [Commander Dace](npcs.md#commander-dace) | Military commander | Loyal but increasingly conflicted | Has never visited a Breaker Station |
| Station Commander Vekk | Station commander | Loyal | Runs Breaker Station Terminus |

#### Resources & Assets

**Wealth:** Moderate (funded by salvaged magitech and sympathizers)
**Territory:** Several hidden facilities (Breaker Stations) and mobile camps
**Assets:**
- The Resonance Key (can shatter a Pylon's crystal matrix)
- 3-4 Breaker Stations across the continent
- Salvaged Old Ones technology repurposed as weapons
- A growing base of popular support among desperate communities

#### Relationship with the Party

**Initial Stance:** Interested and potentially respectful. Sera recognizes the Constant's significance and would prefer the party as allies.

**How to Gain Favor:**
- Express sympathy with the Unravelers' goals (even if disagreeing with methods)
- Help dismantle a Beam safely (the "Third Way")
- Treat Sera's argument with intellectual honesty

**How to Earn Enmity:**
- Destroy a Breaker Station without offering an alternative
- Blindly defend the Beams without engaging with the argument
- Kill Sera or Dace without attempting negotiation

**Benefits of Alliance:**
- Sera's unmatched knowledge of the Beams and Old Ones technology
- Military support from Unraveler forces
- Access to the Resonance Key's capabilities
- Intelligence on all remaining Beam sites

#### Secrets & Intrigue

**Public Doesn't Know:**
- The Breaker Stations exist — most Unraveler recruits are told the Beams are being disrupted through "resonance technology," not human suffering
- Sera has a fragment of the Old Ones' gradual transition protocol. She convinced herself it would take too long, but it exists.

**Internal Conflicts:**
- Commander Dace's growing doubts about the methods
- Several rank-and-file members who would defect if they knew about the Breaker Stations
- Sera's private nightmares about the Breakers — her certainty is a wall she's built to keep functioning

## Minor Factions

### The Remnants

**Type:** Loose Alliance of Survivors
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Leader:** Various settlement leaders, including [Elder Soraya](npcs.md#elder-soraya) (Loom)

#### Quick Description
The Remnants aren't a faction in any organized sense — they're the collective name for the surviving civilian communities scattered across the broken world. United by nothing except the desire to survive another day, the Remnants are pragmatic, resourceful, and deeply suspicious of anyone who claims to have answers.

#### Goals
To survive. To protect their people. To find stability in a world that has none.

#### Relationship to Party
Neutral to cautiously hopeful. The Remnants don't care about cosmic struggles — they care about whether the party will help or harm their community. Earn their trust through action, not ideology.

#### Key NPCs
- [Elder Soraya](npcs.md#elder-soraya) - Leader of Loom, pragmatist

## Faction Interactions & Politics

### Power Dynamics

The Covenant and the Unravelers are locked in a shadow war that most of the world doesn't know exists. The Covenant has knowledge but no strength. The Unravelers have strength but — increasingly — fractured conviction. The Remnants are caught between them, trying to survive while larger forces fight over the shape of reality itself.

### Major Conflicts

#### The Beam War
**Sides:** [Covenant of the Pylon](#the-covenant-of-the-pylon) vs. [The Unravelers](#the-unravelers)
**Cause:** Fundamental disagreement over whether the Beams should be preserved or destroyed
**Status:** The Unravelers are winning. Four Beams have fallen (not all by Unraveler action — some failed naturally). Two remain. The Covenant is desperate.
**How Party Can Influence:** The party's actions determine the fifth Beam's fate and potentially reshape both factions. A diplomatic resolution could create an unprecedented alliance. A violent one could destroy both.

### The Moral Spectrum

Neither faction is wholly right or wrong:

| | Covenant | Unravelers |
|---|---|---|
| **They're right about:** | The Beams prevent catastrophic collapse right now | The Beams were never meant to be permanent |
| **They're wrong about:** | Suppressing the truth to maintain control | The ends justifying the means (Breaker Stations) |
| **Their greatest strength:** | Knowledge and patience | Vision and willingness to act |
| **Their greatest weakness:** | Orthodoxy and institutional cowardice | Fanaticism and moral compromise |
