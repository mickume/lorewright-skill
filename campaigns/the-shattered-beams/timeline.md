# Campaign Timeline

**How to Use This Document**: This provides a scene-level overview for all events in the campaign and when they happen or how long they take. Link chapter titles, NPC names, and location names to their respective documents for easy navigation.

---

# [Chapter 1: The Drawing](chapter-01.md)
**Time:** Day 1

- The PCs are Drawn through malfunctioning Doors at pivotal moments in their lives
- They arrive at [the Waystation](locations.md#the-waystation) and meet [Aldric Vane](npcs.md#aldric-vane)
- Aldric explains the Fracturing, the Ley Beams, and the Constant
- A Thinny breach at the Waystation perimeter forces the party into their first combat (1 Gibbering Mouther, 2 Nothics)
- Aldric sends them toward [Loom](locations.md#loom) to find the Covenant of the Pylon

# [Chapter 2: The Blighted Way](chapter-02.md)
**Time:** Days 2-4 (3 days of travel)

- The party journeys through [the Ashlands](locations.md#the-ashlands)
- **Day 2:** Travel through blighted landscape, environmental storytelling, shared Constant dream during first night's rest
- **Day 3:** Discovery of the Skyship Graveyard (exploration, salvage, log crystal). The Endless Field temporal anomaly (skill challenge)
- **Day 4:** The Ruin of Vethara — activation of [Moth](npcs.md#moth), combat with automated defenses, recovery of the chrono-compass. Loom visible on the horizon by evening.
- **Milestone:** Party reaches Level 5

# [Chapter 3: Loom](chapter-03.md)
**Time:** Days 5-6

- **Day 5:** Arrival at [Loom](locations.md#loom). Meeting with [Elder Soraya](npcs.md#elder-soraya) at the Council Hall. Exploration of the Phasing Market.
- **Day 6:** Contact with Keeper Jorin at the Covenant cell. Intelligence gathering on [Breaker Station Terminus](locations.md#breaker-station-terminus). Optional: discovery of Soraya's secret deal with the Unravelers. Departure south toward the Breaker Station.

# [Chapter 4: The Breaker Station](chapter-04.md)
**Time:** Day 7 (may extend to Day 8 for thorough parties)

- Infiltration or assault of [Breaker Station Terminus](locations.md#breaker-station-terminus)
- Discovery of the Breaker system and the captive psychics
- Meeting [Wren](npcs.md#wren) in the Breaker Chamber
- The moral decision: free the Breakers (surge), leave them (moral cost), or controlled shutdown (DC 18 Arcana)
- Discovery of [Sera Mourne's](npcs.md#sera-mourne-the-threadcutter) identity as the Threadcutter
- Intelligence: the Threadcutter is heading for the [Eld Pylon of Keth](locations.md#the-eld-pylon-of-keth)
- **Milestone:** Party reaches Level 6

# [Chapter 5: The Thinny Storm](chapter-05.md)
**Time:** Days 8-9

- **Day 8:** Return to [Loom](locations.md#loom). The Thinny storm is visible on the horizon. Emergency council with [Elder Soraya](npcs.md#elder-soraya).
- **Day 8-9 (depending on choice):** The storm hits Loom. Defense of the Eastern Gate (3-wave combat) or westward evacuation (skill challenge + combat).
- **The Choice:** Stay to finish saving Loom's people, or leave now to intercept the Threadcutter at the Pylon.
  - If they stay: depart at dawn on Day 9. Arrive at Pylon on Day 10.
  - If they leave early: travel through the night. Arrive at Pylon late on Day 9.

# [Chapter 6: The Eld Pylon](chapter-06.md)
**Time:** Days 9-10 or 10-11 (depending on Chapter 5 choice)

- Arrival at [the Eld Pylon of Keth](locations.md#the-eld-pylon-of-keth)
- Navigation of the Pylon's interior: Entry Hall puzzle, Resonance Chamber hazards, Armory discovery, Memory Banks revelation
- Confrontation with [Commander Dace](npcs.md#commander-dace) in the Control Chamber
- The Old Ones' truth revealed: the Beams were designed as a transitional system
- Pylon defenses activated. Preparation for the Threadcutter's arrival.
- **Milestone:** Party reaches Level 7

# [Chapter 7: The Crimson Threshold](chapter-07.md)
**Time:** Day 10-11 or 11-12 (immediately following Chapter 6)

- [Sera Mourne](npcs.md#sera-mourne-the-threadcutter) arrives at the Eld Pylon with forces and the Resonance Key
- The Parlay: Sera presents her case for destroying the Beam
- If negotiations fail: multi-phase combat (Assault → Surge → The Choice)
- The Beam's fate is decided: preserved, destroyed, or modified (the Third Way)
- Epilogue: consequences ripple outward based on all campaign choices
- Hooks for future campaigns: the sixth Beam, the Tower, new Doors opening

## Background Timeline (Events Before the Campaign)

| When | Event |
|------|-------|
| ~200 years ago | **The Fracturing:** The Divine Gate shatters. Planar boundaries fail. |
| ~200 years ago | The Old Ones' Ley Beams activate automatically as a failsafe, stabilizing reality. |
| ~180 years ago | The Covenant of the Pylon is founded by scholars who discover the Eld Pylons. |
| ~100 years ago | The first Beam falls. The world begins to deteriorate. |
| ~50 years ago | The second and third Beams fall in rapid succession. Thinnies become common. |
| ~30 years ago | [Aldric Vane](npcs.md#aldric-vane) discovers the Waystation and begins maintaining its barrier. |
| ~25 years ago | Aldric briefly joins the proto-Unravelers, then leaves after witnessing early Breaker experiments. |
| 5 years ago | [Sera Mourne](npcs.md#sera-mourne-the-threadcutter) discovers the Beams' transitional nature. The Covenant suppresses her findings. She leaves to found the Unravelers. |
| 3 years ago | The fourth Beam falls. Two remain. Reality deterioration accelerates dramatically. |
| 6 weeks ago | [Wren](npcs.md#wren) is captured and placed in a Breaker chair at Terminus. |
| 1 week ago | The Constant begins Drawing the party members through Doors. |
| Day 1 | The campaign begins. |
